Capture the visible area or the entire page, then save it as PNG, JPG or PDF, copy it, or print it.

Download for Chrome

Whole page, one click

Scrolls and stitches the page, loads lazy content first, and shows sticky headers once. Or grab just what's on screen.

PNG, JPG or PDF

Quality presets, and PDFs split into Letter pages at the quietest row so text isn't cut in half.

Save your way

Save to a folder Chrome remembers, copy to the clipboard, or print.

Mark it up

Highlight, draw, add arrows and text, or black out sensitive details. Filled boxes are burned into the saved image. Burn-in applies to Save, Copy and the Print button, and Ctrl+P with the page focused. Chrome's menu Print, or Ctrl+P pressed from the address bar or find bar, prints unburned, so don't rely on those for redaction.

Install

  1. Download the zip from the latest release and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped JustSave folder.
  4. Optional: to capture in incognito windows, open justSave's Details and turn on Allow in Incognito.

Privacy

No network requests and no analytics. Captures stay in your browser until you save them.
